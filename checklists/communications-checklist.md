# Detection Engineering Checklist

## Detection Goals

- Detect the behavior, not just the signature
- Focus on attacker intent and TTPs
- Minimize false positives

## Required Evidence

- Applicable log sources
- Known good vs suspicious behavior
- Active threat context

## Validation

- Run detection in lab or shadow mode
- Confirm coverage and usefulness
- Tune based on observed noise

## Operationalization

- Add alert routing and ownership
- Create documentation and runbooks
- Review frequency and tuning plan

---

Use this checklist when building hunts and detections from scenario-based investigations.
