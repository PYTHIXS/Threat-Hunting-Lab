# Communications Checklist

## Before Sending a Report

- Confirm who the audience is
- Determine whether the format is technical or executive
- Verify the facts and timeline
- Ensure recommendations are action-oriented

## In the Message

- Summarize the issue clearly
- State the risk and impact
- Include actionable next steps
- Avoid unnecessary jargon

## After Sending

- Track follow-up actions
- Confirm understanding and ownership
- Record decisions, changes, and outcomes

---

Communication quality is essential to effective security investigations and remediation.
