# Exercise 02: Vulnerability Management

## Scenario

A public-facing application has a reported vulnerability with potential remote impact.

## Tasks

1. Identify the affected component
2. Validate exploitability conditions
3. Assess business impact
4. Recommend a priority and remediation approach
5. Document the result in a report

## Deliverables

- Triage summary
- Exploitability assessment
- Risk rating
- Remediation recommendation

---

This exercise focuses on priority, impact, and communication around vulnerabilities.
